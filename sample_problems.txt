1. A shop sells notebooks for ₹25 each. Riya buys 4 notebooks and pays with ₹200. How much change should she receive? Answer: ₹100.
2. A bus has 42 passengers. At the next stop, 15 get off and 8 get on. How many passengers are on the bus? Answer: 35.
3. A recipe uses 3 cups of flour for 12 cookies. How many cups are needed for 20 cookies at the same rate? Answer: 5 cups.
